## Corrections to the rejected pilot

1. Smoothstep per waypoint forces zero velocity at every anchor. Use continuous
   action trajectories, curve tangents and planned stops.
2. Repeated `.87 → 1`/translate/fade entrances flatten different materials. Chat,
   sheet, note, type and character need distinct named-part behavior.
3. Duplicate documents do not demonstrate the promised transformation. Preserve
   the same document actors and gather their cells into the later clean system.
4. Faint shell/pin motion disappears at phone size. Give contact, expressive eyes,
   articulated feet/arms and ground shadow a visible but subordinate response.
5. Poster changes and instantaneous heading swaps replace motion storytelling.
   Let objects/content build, travel and cause the next action before a reading hold.
